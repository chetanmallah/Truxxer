# 🚚 Truxxer - Truck & Vehicle Booking Platform

Truxxer is a modern web application for booking trucks and commercial vehicles for rental, designed for small business owners and logistics needs. With real-time pricing, multiple vehicle options, and automated notifications, Truxxer makes vehicle rental simple, transparent, and efficient.

---

## 🌐 Live Demo

[Visit Truxxer](https://truxer.onrender.com)

---

## Features

- Book trucks and vehicles of various sizes and capacities
- Transparent, real-time pricing based on your rental needs
- All booking data is exportable as Excel sheets for easy business management
- Instant email notifications for every booking
- User-friendly interface, optimized for small business owners

---

## How It Works

1. Select your preferred vehicle from a range of options
2. Enter your rental details and get instant pricing
3. Confirm your booking and receive an email notification
4. All your bookings are securely stored and can be exported as Excel files
